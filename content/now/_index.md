## Health
Needed to change up the work out routine to save time. Switched from 5/3/1 to at-home calisthenics. Still figuring out what routine works best for me. So far, a 10 minute jog in the morning with some mobility work has been a good way to start the day.

Then, in the evenings, I have been alternating upper-lower split EoD with a 5 minute jump rope warm-up followed by a few dynamic stretches.

Upper days look like this:  
Push-ups  
Horizontal pull-ups  
Pike push-ups  
Pull-ups  
Static ring holds  

Lower days:  
Sandbag squats  
Hanging leg raises  
Nordic curl negatives (I can't do nordic curls yet)  
Ring ab rollouts    
Plank  

I am not tracking reps or sets at the moment. Pretty much cycling through each exercise with a warm up set, then a set with 1-2 reps in reserve, followed by a set or two to failure. 

My only workout equipment is gymnastics rings, a couple of 50lb [sandbags](https://link.amazon/B085ACX5v) filled with dirt, a foam roller, a jump rope, and some resistance bands.
