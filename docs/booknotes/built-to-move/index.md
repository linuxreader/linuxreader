## My Notes

There are a lot of exercises and mobility prescriptions to help improve your movement in the book. I left them out of my notes for brevity. 

Sit on the floor for 30 minutes per day.

Breath to maximize CO Tolerance. CO2 prompts hemoglobin to release O2. The more CO2 you can tolerate (the longer and slower your exhales, the more oxygen you'll be able to utilize. 

Do butt squeezes throughout the day. 15 minutes a day can improve hip extension and glute strength. 

Walkers sleep better, have better brain function, raise pain tolerance, have less stress, and enhanced creativity.

Shoulders are the key to a pain-free neck. Strengthening isometrics - not stretching the neck - is how you make your neck feel better.

Eat lots of fruits and vegetables. 

Squatting engages end range tissue which saves that tissue from disuse related thinning and stress.

Contrast therapy is when you cycle between hot and cold to deliver more oxygen and nutrients to muscles and other tissues. Which leads to better recovery.

Practice balance with a slack line, balance board, bosu ball, balance beam, or [Slack Block](https://www.roguefitness.com/slackblock). Get creative.

Create a movement rich environment. Spend less time sitting. Get out of your chair every 30 minutes. 

7 to 9 hours of sleep. Lack of sleep increases pain and pain sensitivity, can cause weight gain, and inflammation. Go to sleep and wake up at the same time every day. No technology before bed. Be careful with caffeine. Exercise and move lots. Don't drink alcohol. Drop body temp before bed. Bedroom should be dark and quiet.
