## 5 Decision Powers
Carpe Momentum: Seize the moment.

Focus on winning each moment. Not the day.

### 1\. **Momentality**
The moment that decisions are made. Your entire life is decided in one moment at a time. Where all decisions are made or not made.

Over an 80 year life you get 252,000,000 ten second moments.

10 seconds is all it takes to change your life or send it to a ditch.

The moment is life's most precious and parishable currency.

8,600 moments each day.

#### The 5 choices you can make each moment
1\. **Neutral flow**
Decisions that rely on routine or habits the keep the status quo.
Sleeping, taking out the trash, etc.

2\. **Power**
Hard choices. Uncomfortable investments in your future self. The seed of winning habits and realized dreams.

3\.**Power Flow**
Hard choice that becomes a good habit. Choosing hard that has become so routine that it is no longer hard.

4\. **Treason**
Feel-good pay later. Easy choices that make your future self bear the consequences. 

Subclass: **Nuclear Treason**
Betrayals so big they can ruin your life in a single catastrophic decision.

A one-way ticket to ruin.

5\. **Treason Flow**
Where dream suffocate and die. Where your health is slowly poisoned. Where your relationships slowly wither from neglect. Easy choices that are hardened into bad habits. Daily, automated treason against the life you could be living. 

#### Command distribution.
**Conscript**
10% power/power flow - Minimal growth or investment.
60% Treason -- Constant indulgence
30% Neutral - Just existing
Result: A life of debt, disease, and regret.

**Commander**
60% Power/Power Flow - Dominant investment in the future.
30% Neutral - Maintenance of the machine.

Everything significant starts insignificantly. Big things are built with small actions. 

### 2\. **Eventuality**
Where you suffer or thrive from decisions. You live in the skin you sew. Where all of the easy decisions force you to live a hard life. Or where hard decisions let you live an easy life.

### 3\. **Pastuality**
The past moments that influence your current behavior. How we interpret the past can serve or sabotage us.

The sands of the past lie dormant until you revive them to either server or sabotage you.

Rear view mirrors are for glances, not gazes.

If you want to control your future, look ahead, not behind. Start owning the past. Or let it own you.

#### **Sabotage pastuality**
We narrate the past to justify easy choices and to dismiss hard choices. 

#### **Service pastuality**
The practice of reframing memories to strengthen you in the moment to encourage a better future. Rather then letting memories become an unexamined liability.

##### **R.A.D.A.R**
The antidote to Sabotage pastuality.

Reveal > Audit > Disprove > Adjust > Repeat

**R.** Reveal the big ugly
You can't rewrite a story you've buried. Dig up the big ugly memory that has you in paper handcuffs.

**A.** Audit the story
Crack open the decision corpse and expose the disease of easy.

What emotions stomped on logic? 
Did you buy the hype?
Where were the blind spots? Did you skip your due diligence?

Examine overlooked assumptions that led to the ambush. By the end of this audit, you should clearly see which impulses and blind spots led to your downfall.

**D.** Disprove
Reveal yourself as a liar. Find proof that your self-criticism is just an emotional opinion. Find stories that challenge your rigid beliefs. Find case studies who prove that mistakes aren't life sentences. Every "lessons learned" chips away at the myth you've cursed yourself with. 

**A.** Adjust with "next time" or "new meaning".
Transform sabotage into service. What did you learn and what will your change?

Reclaim your power by choosing a new meaning. Accept the truth, then find the higher purpose buried in the wreckage.

Acknowledge the loss. Declare the account closed, then burn it.

**R.** Repeat
Run **RADAR** indefinitely.
